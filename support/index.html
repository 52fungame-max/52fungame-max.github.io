Parent Support

For caregivers using First Words: Toddler Play on iPhone or iPad.

Restore a purchase

  1. On the home screen, open Settings (the grown-up area).
  2. Answer the two math questions.
  3. Tap Restore Purchase and sign in with the Apple ID that bought the unlock.

The unlock is a one-time non-consumable item. If you use Family Sharing, Apple’s Family Sharing rules apply.

What is free without buying

  • Scratch & Learn: the Things That Go theme
  • Listen & Find: the Ocean Animals theme

Other themes unlock with the full-content purchase.

The app will not go online to play

Installed cards, art, and audio work offline. You only need the internet to buy or restore, or to open the privacy/support pages.

Contact

Email 52fungame@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and what happened. Do not include a child’s name or photos.

Billing, refunds, and Apple ID issues are handled by Apple: Apple billing support.

家长支持（中文）

恢复购买

  1. 在首页打开设置（家长区域）。
  2. 连续答对两道算术题。
  3. 点“恢复购买”，使用当时付款的 Apple ID 登录。

未购买时可以玩什么

  • 刮卡：Things That Go（会动的东西）
  • 听一听：Ocean Animals（海洋动物）

联系我们

请发邮件到 52fungame@gmail.com， 写明机型、iOS 版本和遇到的问题。请不要发送孩子的姓名或照片。退款与账单请走 Apple 账单支持。
